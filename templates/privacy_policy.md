# Reflection Elite: Privacy Policy

> **Mandatory Policy Page for Razorpay KYC & Data Protection**  
> **Brand:** Reflection Elite | Website: https://reflectionelite.com  

---

## 1. Information We Collect
When you purchase a mirror or browse our website, we collect necessary transaction details:
- **Contact Details:** Name, email address, phone number, shipping address, and billing address.
- **Order History:** Products purchased, dimensions, custom specifications, and invoice records.
- **Device Information:** IP address, browser type, and cookies to ensure smooth cart and checkout functionality.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
We use your information exclusively to:
- Process and fulfill your mirror orders.
- Provide live wooden crate freight tracking updates via SMS and WhatsApp.
- Send automated order confirmation and tax invoice emails.
- Comply with statutory tax, invoicing, and GST record-keeping regulations.

## 3. Data Protection & Non-Disclosure
We **never sell, rent, or trade** your personal contact information to third-party marketing companies. Your delivery address is shared strictly with our freight logistics carriers (e.g. Blue Dart, Delhivery) solely for doorstep delivery.

## 4. Payment Security
All online payments are encrypted and processed by **Razorpay Payment Gateway**, a PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment processor. We never store CVV numbers, card numbers, or banking credentials.
