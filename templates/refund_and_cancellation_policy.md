## 1. Overview
At **Reflection Elite**, every mirror is handcrafted with precision, premium glass, and bespoke craftsmanship. Because mirrors are fragile luxury items transported in custom-built wooden protective crates, we have established clear, fair policies to protect our customers during transit.

---

## 2. Transit Damage & Replacement Guarantee
We guarantee that your mirror will arrive safely. In the rare event of damage during transit:

1. **Mandatory Unboxing Video:**  
   Customers **must record an unboxing video** starting before opening the outer wooden crate and inspect the mirror glass in the video.
2. **Reporting Window:**  
   Any transit damage, crack, or defect must be reported to our support team via WhatsApp or email within **48 hours of delivery**.
3. **Resolution:**  
   Upon verifying the unboxing video, Reflection Elite will immediately dispatch a **100% free replacement mirror** or issue a full refund at no extra cost to you.

---

## 3. Custom & Made-to-Order Mirrors
- Mirrors made to bespoke dimensions, custom frames, or personalized finishes enter the glass cutting and silvering process within **24 hours of order confirmation**.
- Cancellations for custom bespoke mirrors are accepted within 24 hours of placing the order for a full refund. After 24 hours, custom orders cannot be canceled.

---

## 4. Cancellation Policy
- **Standard Orders:** You may cancel your order anytime before it has been dispatched from our workshop. A full 100% refund will be issued to your original payment method.
- **After Dispatch:** If an order has already been dispatched, standard shipping and return logistics fees may apply if the package is recalled.

---

## 5. Refund Processing Timelines
- Approved refunds are credited back to your original payment method (Bank Account, UPI, Credit/Debit Card) within **5 to 7 business days** via our secure payment partner, Razorpay.
