# Reflection Elite: Shipping & Delivery Policy

---

## 1. Domestic Shipping Coverage
Reflection Elite delivers handcrafted luxury mirrors pan-India to all serviceable pincodes across metro cities and tier 1/2/3 towns through specialized fragile freight logistics partners (Blue Dart, Delhivery, Safechem Fragile Cargo).

---

## 2. Reinforced Wooden Crate Packaging
To prevent glass breakage, every Reflection Elite mirror undergoes a 4-layer packaging process:
1. Anti-scratch bubble wrap and foam edge protectors.
2. High-density shock-absorbing thermocol cushions.
3. Heavy-duty 5-ply corrugated inner box.
4. Outer reinforced wooden crate bolted for maximum transit security.

---

## 3. Order Processing & Dispatch Timelines
- **In-Stock Mirrors:** Dispatched within **2 to 4 business days**.
- **Handcrafted / Bespoke Made-to-Order Mirrors:** Hand-assembled, inspected, and dispatched within **7 to 10 business days**.
- Customers receive live SMS/Email tracking updates as soon as the wooden crate is handed over to the courier partner.

---

## 4. Transit Timelines
- **Metro Cities (Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata):** 3 to 6 business days after dispatch.
- **Rest of India:** 5 to 9 business days after dispatch.

---

## 5. Shipping Charges
- **Standard Shipping:** Flat-rate or Free Shipping applied automatically at checkout based on order value thresholds.
- Any applicable local octroi or state entry permits for oversized freight are coordinated directly by our dispatch team.
